Financial Independence Settings
ⓘ
This section helps estimate when you might reach Financial Independence (FI).
• FI Multiple: How many years of expenses you need saved (e.g., 25 means a 4% withdrawal rate).
• Expected Return: Your estimated average annual investment return after inflation.

Small changes compound: a 1% higher expected return — or trimming annual expenses — can bring your FI date forward by years. Adjust these and watch "Years to FI" on the Savings tab respond.
