# C2 withholding engine

- New `src/calc/withholding.js` (pure): `weeklyEquivalent`, `withholdingForPay({gross, cycle, fy, claimsTaxFree, hasStudyDebt, extraPerPay})` returning whole-dollar `{x, tax, help, total, totalCombined, scale}`, `expectedPay(source, fy)`, plus `roundHalfUp`, `weeklyFromRows`, `isExactHalf` (used by tests).
- Integer arithmetic: coefficients times 10^4, x in cents, round half up on 10^-6 dollars. Weekly, fortnightly (weekly then times 2), monthly (x3/13, 33c rule, result times 13/3 rounded) and quarterly; Scale 1 and 2 plus the Schedule 8 study-loan component. `fy` is an income-year key or an ISO date.
- `total` is the component sum (tax + help + extra); `totalCombined` adds the Schedule 1 and 8 coefficients and rounds once (published combined `b` values differ by A$0.0001 in two rows, never changes a whole dollar in the tested cases). Match tolerance (A$2 per fortnight, FT-R-75) is C5's job.
- Examples: 2025-26 A$626 + A$72 = A$698; 2026-27 A$616 + A$58 = A$674, combined A$672.
- Tests: 42 assertions in `withholding.test-cases.js` (W1-W4, D1-D6, Y1-Y5, O1-O8). O8 sweeps x = 0.99 to 4000.99 over scale 1, scale 2 and both study-loan tables of both years: 32,008 values, no differences, no exact .50 cases occur.
