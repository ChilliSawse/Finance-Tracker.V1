# P2 - toast stays off an open sheet

**What changed.** While a sheet is open (`body.has-sheet`) the toast no longer sits over the form. Under 768px it docks in a strip at the top of the screen and the sheet is shortened by 84px to keep that strip clear (the Save/Cancel bar and fields are untouched). From 768px it sits in the dimmed area to the left of the 440px side panel. Role, aria-live and the Undo button are unchanged and reachable. Rules live in `src/ui/sheet.css` (the base toast rules in `style.css` were not touched).

**Why.** At 390 wide the undo toast ("Added Family car. Undo") covered the fields and hint text being edited.

**Tests added (1).** `P2.1` in `src/ui/sheet.test-cases.js` (browser only): renders the repo CSS in iframes of 390, 1440 and 800 wide with an open sheet and a toast with Undo, and checks the toast is inside the viewport and clear of the sheet header, body and footer, with role/aria-live kept. It fails on the old CSS (390 and 800). run-cases 2430/2430 (unchanged, browser case skipped there), tests.html 2840/2840.

**Later.** The toast's base position still lives in `style.css`; moving it into `sheet.css` would be tidier once the dead-code clean-up there lands.
