# H8 Today "Savings rate (actual)" = what was moved into saving buckets (owner ruling FT-R-118, M-11)

Chilli's ruling (2026-11-22, with FT-R-116): the actual figure is what was really moved into savings buckets in the last complete pay, from transactions, shown as a percent of that pay's income and the dollar amount. It was (income minus spending) over income.

## The data source, and why

Money actually moved is only on record as a **transaction**. So the figure counts rows in the last complete pay that are all of: outgoing (negative), a transfer (category `transfer`), and carry a `bucketId` that is a bucket of kind `save` (or a goal under one). Sum of those over that pay's Income-category credits.

Not used, because they record what was meant to happen, not what did:
- Bank-transfers card ticks (`savingModel.transfers.bankSetTo`): this is the amount a standing order is set to, not a payment.
- Pay-day auto-credit and `savingModel.movements` (`calc/pay-roll.js`): assumes the split was moved; movements are capped to 24 months too.
- Bucket `currentBalance` changes: written by auto-credit, the Move action and the user's own balance edits, so they mix assumed and real.
- Any row with a `bucketId` that is not a transfer (a purchase charged to Fire is spending, not saving). Incoming rows are ignored so a pair of transfers (out of spending, into savings) is not counted twice.

## What shows

- Enough: label "Savings rate (actual)", value "27%", caption "A$950 last pay, 17 Oct to 30 Oct" (`a.today.rateCapActual`).
- Existing conditions kept: last complete pay cycle (month in month mode), history reaches that cycle's first day, income landed in it. Failing those still shows the planned figure as before.
- Conditions met but no transfer in that pay is marked to a saving bucket: "Not enough to tell yet" with the hint "Mark each transfer to a savings bucket to see it". No percent is shown, since saving nothing and not recording it look the same.

## Needs a design call (UNVERIFIED in use)

The app has no screen yet that lets a transfer row be marked to a bucket: quick-add records spending and income only, and `effectiveBucket` ignores transfers. The calculation honours a `bucketId` on a transfer row (CSV or manual), but until a "this transfer went to <bucket>" control exists (Activity row edit, or an import rule), most real data will show "Not enough to tell yet". The sample household has no transfers, so it shows that message too.
