# F5 Asset and debt sheets (UI)

- `src/ui/plan-assets-debts.js` (M0's stub, filled; pure, no DOM):
  - Factories `newAsset(list, fields)`, `newDebt(list, fields)`, `newIncomeSourceRow(list, fields)`: every new row gets its id (`a-<slug>`, `l-<slug>`, `inc-<slug>`, `-2` on collision) and every F2 detail field at creation, because the `assetDebtVersion` guard stops the migration filling rows added later. A named asset or debt with no kind or label is guessed with F2's `suggestKind` / `suggestLabel` and flagged (`kindSuggested` / `labelSuggested`); a blank or default name is plain cash / unlabelled. An unset asset `interestRate` stays absent (S1.8).
  - `assetSheetSpec(asset, {today, kind, carry})` and `debtSheetSpec(debt, {carry})`: asset sheet by kind (cash: rate and how often it is paid; invested: super tick, total return, dividend yield and frequency, reinvest; appreciating: growth; depreciating: purchase price, date, method, rate, life, residual), debt sheet (rate, variable or fixed with fixed-until and rate after, compounding, repayment frequency, minimum, good or bad label). `openSheet` has no conditional fields, so `reopenOn` (`kind`, `interestType`) makes `plan.js` reopen the sheet with what was typed so far.
  - "use A$X": a plausibility note on the balance, with a fix button, when `depreciatedValue` differs from the entered balance by more than 5% (F2's `depreciationDiffers`). The balance changes only on the tap.
  - `applyAssetValues` / `applyDebtValues`: a kind or label picked or changed on the sheet confirms it; saving with the suggestion untouched keeps it flagged.
  - The "Check these N" card (`checkItems`, `checkCardHtml`, `confirmSuggestion`): Confirm one, Confirm all (only the flag clears, kind, label and figures stay) or Change (opens the sheet); one Undo.
  - Chips for the Plan rows: kind chip (Super, Cash, Invested, Grows, Loses value; "(suggested)" in the review colour), good or bad chip plus `rateBand` (High, Moderate, Low interest, same cut-offs as the old Liabilities card).
- `plan-lists.js` (Assets and Debts rows only): rows carry the chips; `debtAssetSheetSpec(kind, row, opts)` passes the F5 spec through (validate, plausibility, reopenOn); `saveDebtAsset` builds new rows with the factories and writes edits through `apply*Values`; `newIncomeSource(list)` now goes through the factory (id).
- `plan.js` (minimal): sheet reopen on kind change and the depreciation offer (`today`), the Check card appended in `#plan-review`, its click handler, `addIncome` passes the list.
- Creation sites routed through the factories: `settings-events.js` `addAsset`, `addLiability`, `addIncomeSource` (id via `newIncomeSourceId`), setup `makeDebt` (C7's debts step, `minRepayment` null when blank, was 0), setup quiz `blankSource`, and the default data income source (`inc-new-income-source`). `asset-debt-migration.js` gains `newAssetId` and `newIncomeSourceId` (same id helper as the migration, one owner).
- `plan.css`: Check card list layout. Strings `f.ad.*` in `en-f.js`.
- Tests: new group `asset-debt-sheets` (`plan-assets-debts.test-cases.js`): F5.1 to F5.6 (factories and every creation site carry id and F2 fields, specs by kind, the offer needs a tap, add/edit/delete/undo and flags, the Check card, chips; hostile names escaped), 6 cases, 74 assertions. One existing assertion in A9b.4 named the stub fallback (`['name','balance','interestRate']`); it now checks those are the first three fields of the F5 sheet (the stub it described is gone).
- `run-cases` 2007 to 2081; `tests.html` 2445 of 2445 (test:ci).
- Screenshots (scratchpad `w13-F5`): Check card, Plan Debts and Assets with chips, asset sheet with the "use A$" offer, debt sheet (variable and fixed), light and dark at 1440 and 390.
