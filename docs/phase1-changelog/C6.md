# C6 Salary and Set-hours layouts, withholding questions, payslip check panel

- `src/ui/income-layout.js` filled: `openIncomeSheet(index)` opens the Salary or Set-hours sheet (D1 `openSheet`) and returns true; Variable, Self-employed and missing sources return false and keep their editors. Today's "Set your pay day" nudge now opens it.
- Fields: name, salary (a year or per payslip) or hourly rate and hours a week, pay cycle, "next pay day" (stored as `payAnchor`, the pay before it; no second field, X-9), super % (shows 12 when nothing is stored), the four withholding questions, salary sacrifice. Medicare exemption and salary sacrifice answered Yes show "Not modelled yet" (FT-R-67). A link switches between the two layouts.
- "What we work out" (from `withholdingForPay` and `calculateTotals`): gross, tax + HELP taken out, take-home, tax for the year, estimated refund or bill, super, tax tables year.
- "Check against a payslip" panel (gross, tax, net, date, or upload): `checkPayslip` result with expected line, likely-cause copy and one-tap fixes that set the sheet's answers (Save keeps them). A differing gross is only offered, through `confirmDialog` (S3). Diagnosed example: A$626 + A$72 = A$698 matches; with HELP off, "The A$72 difference is about what HELP takes on your pay." with "I have a HELP debt".
- `settings-forms.js`: employee rows show a one-line summary and "Edit pay"; a source flagged `migrationPrompt:'payslip-job'` shows the switch offer (`acceptPayslipJob`, then opens the Salary sheet; "Not now" clears the flag for good).
- `settings-events.js` (income routing only): click routing for the new buttons, `basis`/`withholding` kept in step when the type changes, a new income source carries the C4 fields and opens its sheet.
- `pdf-ui.js`: new exported `readPayslip(file, onStatus, onError)` (parse only, writes nothing); settings autofill now appends the check result to its status line.
- New source field `salarySacrifice` (boolean, asked only). No payslip figure is a literal in `src/`.
- Strings `c.income.*` in `en-c.js`; `income-layout.css` (tokens only); test group `income-layout` (C6.1 to C6.6, 41 assertions). `run-cases` 1315 to 1356; `tests.html` 1660 to 1701.
- Note: `sheet.css` sets `display` on `.sheet-note`, which beats `hidden`; `income-layout.css` re-hides empty notes inside the sheet.
