# A11 Help

- New `src/ui/help.js` and `help.css`: one "?" button per screen (Today, Activity, Plan, Future, Settings, Setup). Each opens one read-only help sheet through `openSheet`: "What this is for" and "How to". Words are `a.help.*` in `en-a.js`; the sheet is built with `textContent`.
- `openSheet` gains two options, `content` (extra DOM blocks above the fields) and `readOnly` (no Save, Cancel reads Close). Existing callers are unchanged.
- Buttons: `mountHelpButtons()` (called from `main.js`) adds Today, Activity, Plan and Future; Settings mounts its own in `buildShell`. Future's text describes the finished screen (net worth path, FI, What If) in two lines.
- **For C7:** the Setup screen's "?" comes from `mountHelpButton('setup', headerEl)` (exported from `help.js`; safe to call twice). Setup help text is already in `en-a.js`.
- Deleted the "How to use" info panels (5 were left in `index.html`: Income, Expenses, Savings, Liabilities, What If; the other ten had gone in earlier steps), `setupInfoSections` and its helpers in `tabs.js`, and the dead `.info-section`, `.info-toggle`, `.info-dismiss` CSS. `.info-more` and `.info-tip` stay (still used). `ft-info-collapsed` is kept by name in `RETIRED_KEYS` (`backup.js`) so Erase all clears it.
- Empty states: Today (Open Plan), Activity (Add a transaction) and the Plan lists already had one action. The Plan Debts, Assets and Categories placeholders had none; they now offer "Open Future" / "Open Settings" until A9b fills them.
- Settings > About, Licence line now reads "Ledger will be free and open source (AGPL-3.0) at its public launch."
