# H5 A pay rise in What If moved only the long-range numbers (live bug fix)

Reported on iPhone after 2.0.0: a "I got a pay rise" scenario with the salary set to 150,000 changed only "years to financial independence". Take-home, tax, per-pay amounts, the savings split and the FI number stayed put.

## What was and was not wired (before the fix, his data with the income switched to Salary)

| Figure | Moved |
|---|---|
| Take-home per pay, each bucket per pay (Important, Splurge, Smile, Fire, Grow) | no |
| FI number (a year of Daily + Splurge) | no |
| Net worth in 1, 5, 10, 20 years, years to FI | yes (these read the gross, super and the withholding engine) |
| Savings rate | no, by design (see below) |
| Debts, HELP balance, Mojo | no, by design (not income-driven) |

The sample household (no payslip figures stored) moved everything except the savings rate, before and after.

## Root cause

`calculateTotals` reads a stored `taxRemoved` / `invoicedPayPostTax` (per pay) as the truth for an employee source. His source was migrated from Self-employed, and accepting the "switch to Salary" offer keeps those figures on purpose (C4/C8: "payslip pay kept"). A scenario sets only `grossAnnual`, so the overrides still said "tax X, pay Y", and take-home, tax, every per-pay bucket and the FI number stayed on the old payslip. The long-range path and FI years read the gross directly, so only those moved. The same stale-payslip effect hit a real salary edit in the Salary sheet (`applyIncomeValues` wrote the new gross and left the old payslip figures).

## Fix

- New `src/calc/income-rebase.js`: `rebasePayslipFigures` moves the stored payslip tax by the withholding engine's tax difference between the old and new gross, and the stored pay by the gross difference less that tax difference. The payslip's own calibration (HELP, Medicare, offsets) is kept and the rise still shows. Employee sources only; a source with no payslip figures is untouched; an unchanged gross changes nothing.
- `resolveAt` (`src/calc/scenario.js`) calls it once after the changes apply, so every consumer (cards, Show all numbers, Compare, the Future chart, FI path, goal plan, the Answer) sees the same corrected source. The real data is never changed.
- `applyIncomeValues` (`src/ui/income-layout.js`) calls it when a saved Salary sheet changes the gross, so a real raise and "Make this real" move take-home too.

## By design, left alone

- Savings rate: the split is a share of take-home, so a rise in pay raises every save bucket in proportion and the rate stays the same.
- A pay change on a Self-employed or Variable source is inert for take-home: those types read take-home and tax per pay, not a gross. Not changed (it would be a design choice).

## Tests

- `scenario-results` "H5": a pay rise on a source with payslip figures moves take-home, per-pay buckets, net worth in 10 and 20 years and FI years; payslip tax and pay rise by the gross difference; the real data is untouched; an unchanged gross leaves the payslip alone. Fails on the old code.
- `income-layout` "H5": saving a new salary moves the stored payslip tax and pay; an unchanged salary does not; a source without payslip figures gets none. Fails on the old code.
- run-cases 3027 to 3044 passed, 0 failed.
