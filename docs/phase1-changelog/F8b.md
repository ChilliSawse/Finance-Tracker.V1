# F8b Answer (bottom half), Compare and Make this real

- `src/ui/scenario/results.js`: one model for the result cards, Show all numbers and Compare. `buildResultSet(data, changes, today)` works out Now and the scenario (read at the last start date among the changes) from F1 (`resolveAt`), F3 (`fiSummary`, `savingsRatePlanned`), F4 (`planGoalsFromData`) and the split (`getBuckets`, `netPerPayCents`, the same engines F4 uses). Rows: take-home, each bucket a pay, savings rate, a row per goal (on time or short a pay, from F4's `perGoal`), goals need / short / extra take-home needed (F4), Mojo, debts, HELP, net worth in 1, 5, 10 and 20 years, years to FI and FI number (F3). `diffOf` and `bestOf` give the "A$X more" chips and the Best word. Cards are tinted by bucket role (tokens only). No amount or date is typed in code.
- `explain.js`: Explain this number lines per row (F4 `explainRequired`, F3 `explainFi`, the split), opened in a read-only sheet. Each card and each table row has the button.
- `compare.js`: exactly two columns, Now and this scenario; the six preset chips (Everyday, Goals, Safety, Debt, Decision default, Long range; no add or remove); "Best on each row" as a toggle that prints the word Best. No picker, no "Add a scenario to compare", no saved scenarios as columns, no Advanced view.
- `make-real.js`: the "Happy with this?" bar under the Answer and the button in the Compare step bar. One actionable change goes straight to its step; more open a list: "Starting now" with Apply all now, "Starting later" with one Apply each (nothing is scheduled). Plan, goal, split and FI steps go through F1's `applyMakeReal` with its one combined undo and an Undo toast; pay and loan steps open the normal sheet pre-filled (`openIncomeSheet` with `prefill`, a rate for an hourly source, the loan sheet from F5/E's `debtAssetSheetSpec`) and show an Undo toast after Save. A new income source is added like Plan's "Add income" and removed again if the sheet is cancelled. Steps made real are marked Applied for the visit so a scaled change is never applied twice. End dates are dropped with a note; missing targets, moved real figures and one-offs are noted.
- Registry: after the merge F8a's multi-registration in `index.js` is kept; I only added `'make-real'` to its accepted names and drew its renderers in the Compare step-bar slot. The Answer bottom registers at order 10 (after F8a's -10), Compare and Make this real with no order. The bottom half reads F8a's `getAnswer` and redraws on `ft:answer-change`: the chosen option sets the Splurge, Smile and take-home "after" figures from F4's plan.
- `income-layout.js` (C6, minimal): `openIncomeSheet(index, {prefill, onSaved})`; `prefill` overlays sheet values, `onSaved(source)` runs after a save.
- `main.js`: the css import and the `results.js` import (registers the views). `en-f.js`: one block `// F8b` at the end (`f.res.*`).
- Tests (group `scenario-results`): F8b.1 to F8b.8 pure, F8b.9 browser (escaping, two columns, six chips). run-cases 2713 after the merge (F8b adds 79); tests.html 3129 of 3129; sweep: all 25 screens ok.
- Left for later: one actionable change that is a plan item or goal applies directly with Undo rather than through its own sheet; the Applied marks live in memory for the visit; savings "Best" is the higher figure, bucket-per-pay rows have no Best; F8a's `Plan for this` and the top of the Answer are separate.
- Known quirk: an hourly pay source is pre-filled as a rate rounded to the cent, so the saved gross can differ from the scenario's by a few dollars a year. F1's message says "1 changes applied" for one change.
