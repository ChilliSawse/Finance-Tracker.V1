# H7 Savings = pay going into savings buckets (owner ruling FT-R-116)

Chilli's ruling (2026-11-22): "savings rate" means the share of take-home pay going into buckets of kind `save`. Take-home minus spending is not savings, because unspent money may roll into the next pay. Every savings rate shows the dollar amount beside it.

## What changed

- One function, `savingsPlanned(data, opts)` in `src/calc/fi.js`, returns `{ratePct, perPayCents, perYearCents}`: top-level `save` buckets over the sum of top-level buckets' per-pay amounts; per year = per pay x pays a year (`planTotals().cyclesPerYear`). `savingsRatePlanned` is now a thin wrapper (same number as before). `fiSummary` carries `savings` as well as `savingsRate`.
- Today, planned: `savingsRateFigure` uses `savingsPlanned`. Label "Savings rate (planned)", value "29%", caption "A$997 a pay into savings" (was "planned from your split").
- Future FI card: "Savings rate  29% · A$997 a pay"; sub line "Pay going into Smile, Fire extinguisher and the other saving buckets: A$25,926 a year".
- What If cards, Show all numbers, Compare: "29% · A$997.15 a pay". With a pay rise the difference reads "same rate, A$50.24 more a pay"; Compare's Best marker uses the amount when the rate ties.
- Explain: "The buckets that save add up to {save} of {net} a pay, which is {rate}%. That is {year} a year." then "Savings means pay that goes into your saving buckets. Money you do not spend is not counted, because it can roll into your next pay."
- Milestones toast and feed: "...your savings rate hit 20% (A$540 a pay)." Feed text now says "you're putting X% of your pay into savings".
- Strings are in `en.js`, `en-a.js`, `en-f.js`.

## Snapshots and milestones (history is not rewritten)

- Snapshots (`ft-snapshots`): new entries use the new rate and add `savingsDef: 'buckets'` and `savingsPerPayCents`. Older entries are left exactly as they were; no `savingsDef` marks them as the old net-minus-expenses figure. Nothing reads the stored rate for display today. A snapshot already written today is overwritten as before.
- Milestones (`ft-milestones`): the stored "celebrated" threshold came from the old definition. The first run after this change sets the threshold from the new figure without announcing anything and records `savingsDef`; later crossings celebrate as usual and log the amount. Past feed events are left as they were.

## Legacy `calculateTotals().savingsRate`

Kept (net minus planned expenses over net) and commented as legacy. Used by nothing displayed now; only tests read it. Not used by exports, reports or the FI calc.

## Today "actual" (unchanged, pending a design call)

Label "Savings rate (actual)", caption "last pay, {from} to {to}". `savingsRateFigure` in `src/ui/today.js`: for the last complete pay cycle of transactions, (income minus spending) over income, spending excluding transfers; shown only when history reaches that cycle's first day and income landed in it. This is the take-home minus spending idea the ruling rejects. It has no dollar amount yet.
