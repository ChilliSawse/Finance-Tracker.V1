# H9 "Went to": mark a transfer to a bucket, and auto-suggest it on import (owner ruling FT-R-119, M-11)

Closes the gap H8 left open: nothing in the UI could set the `bucketId` that Today's "Savings rate (actual)" reads on a transfer row.

## What the user sees
- **Quick-add** has a third choice, "Transfer" (Spending / Income / Transfer). A transfer has an amount, a note and a date, no category chips, and a "Went to" list: every top-level bucket, the goals under a saving bucket ("goal in Smile") and "Somewhere else". Save stays off until one is chosen ("Choose where it went, or Somewhere else."). Button: "Save A$400.00 to Fire". Line under the amount: "Moved into Fire. It counts toward your savings rate, not as spending."
- **Activity**: a transfer row shows "Transfer · between your accounts" and a pill "Went to Fire". Tapping the row opens the same Quick-add in edit mode with the same "Went to" list (set it, change it, or pick Somewhere else). A transfer with no bucket shows "Not marked to a bucket" and "Choose where it went".
- **Import** (CSV, XLSX, PDF): an outgoing row whose description names a bucket's "bank account name" becomes a transfer to that bucket, marked suggested (FT-R-117). Preview shows "Will be added as a transfer to Smile (suggested). Pick a category to change that." Summary adds "1 looked like a transfer to one of your buckets. Check it in To review."
- A suggested row shows "Went to Smile (suggested)", stays in To review, and has a one-tap "Confirm" (with Undo). Opening it and saving also confirms it, and changing the bucket clears the flag.

## Data
`bucketSuggested: true` on the transaction (absent once confirmed or set by hand). `bucketId` on a transfer row means where it went. No migration: rows are stored whole, so the flag rides through IndexedDB, backup export/import (`sanitizeTransactionRows` keeps unknown fields) and the boot recovery bundle (U3). Tested in all three.

## Matching rule (`src/calc/transfers.js`)
Case, punctuation and extra spaces ignored; accents folded; the label must appear in the description as whole words (so "Fire" matches "Transfer to FIRE", not "FIRESTONE TYRES"). A label under 3 letters or digits is ignored. Several buckets match: the longest label wins ("Fire Fund" over "Fire"); a tie between different buckets gives no suggestion. Never overrides a category from a learned rule or one picked in the preview (preview no longer writes the automatic category onto the draft, so a pick can be told apart; stored result is unchanged).

## Do suggested rows count? Yes
Savings rate (actual) counts a suggested, unconfirmed transfer. It stays in To review. Confirming changes no number; changing it to a spending bucket or Somewhere else removes it.

## Transfers are not spending
`categoryId 'transfer'` rows are now skipped in: left this pay and the essential, non-essential and unsorted splits (`calc/pay-cycle.js`), spend cache month, year, income and cycle totals, Today by-category and by-day, Activity day totals, Where it went and spent total, the Essential and Non-essential filters, monthly review spend, and average monthly expenses. Rows stay listed and stay in the cache for H8. A transfer has no category object; Activity names it "Transfer".

## Tests
New group `transfers` (`src/calc/transfers.test-cases.js`, 8 cases) plus cases in quick-add, activity, today and transactions (browser: store, export, restore). run-cases 3459 passed, 0 failed (baseline 3378); test:ci 3961 (baseline 3876); phase1-gate, tools-check, sweep --full ok. Browser check at 390 (touch) and 1440, synthetic sample household: transfer added with "Went to Fire", Today's actual went from "Not enough to tell yet" to 12% / A$400 last pay, synthetic CSV matched and was suggested (19% / A$650 with it counted), one-tap confirm, edit of a transfer from Activity.

## Not verified / design calls
- Activity and To review show only the current pay, so a suggested transfer from a statement for the LAST pay (the one Today counts) is not listed there to confirm. It still counts. Needs a design call (a wider range for To review, or a "Suggested transfers" list). Existing behaviour for any imported review row.
- Account numbers as bank labels ("1234 5678" vs "12345678") are not matched; only whole words.
- Goals nested under goals are not offered or counted (H8 counts one level only).
- The iPhone, and real bank statements.
