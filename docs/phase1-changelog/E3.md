- New `src/calc/smile.js`: `smilePlan` (paysLeft, per-pay needed, share needed, readyOn, status done / on-track / late / impossible / no-date, lateDays),
  `suggestGoalShares` (smallest 5% moves from on-time dated goals to late ones; `{kind:'raise'}` when slack is short; null when nobody is late),

- New `src/calc/review.js`: `reviewDue(data, today, {hasTransactions})`, `buildMonthlyReview` (spending, movements, targets, transfers), `inMonth`, `monthRange`, `previousMonth`.

  review state at `data.review.lastOpenedMonth`; allocation rows read `percentage`, `role`, `name`, `bankLabel`. An unseeded `bankSetTo` gives an empty card.
- `reviewDue` is null on the last day of a month (the month is not complete) and gives the month on the 1st. U28: `to: month-31` includes the 30th of a 30-day month (tested as a string bound).
- U27: on pays every second Friday from 2025-11-14 (today 2025-11-13) the mock's ready dates reproduce (Concert 12 Dec, bike 17 Apr, Japan 20 Feb) and the hint is "5% from Japan to the bike".
