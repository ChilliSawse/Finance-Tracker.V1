# E4 bucket schema, migration and the dry-run script

- `src/state/bucket-migration.js`: `migrateBucketModel` (M0 to M9) behind `savingModel.version >= 1`. Roles (first match per role,
  duplicates get null and are listed as unmapped), slots (core 1 to 5, Grow 6, others 7 to 10), kinds, the Mojo row at 0%, liability ids
  (`l-<slug>`), the Fire list from E2's `defaultFireTargets`, legacy Smile goal into a child goal (`g-mig-smile`), legacy Fire goal into a
  `saver` target after the debts (`t-mig-fire`, FT-R-82), Mojo goal into a custom ladder step, bounds check and own-template fork (FT-R-85),
  `autoCredit.on=false` (FT-R-80), `legacyAllocation` copy, `bankSetTo` seeded to today's per-pay cents.
- Balance-sum check (buckets, goals saved, saver targets, in cents) before against after. A mismatch throws, so the chain returns
  `{aborted:true}` and nothing is written. An unedited placeholder or empty allocation becomes the template at 60 / 10 / 10 / 20.
- `afterImport` moves a stale `autoCredit.rolledThrough` to the latest pay date (never back).
- `defaults.js`: a fresh allocation is the template, `savingModel` (auto-credit on), `bucketGoals: []`, placeholder liability id.
  `settings-events.js addLiability`: new liabilities get an id (`newLiabilityId`).
- `sample-data.js legacySampleFinanceData` drops `savingModel` and `bucketGoals` (fresh defaults carry them now); `chain.test-cases.js`
  `settledData` carries `savingModel: {version: 1}` (fixture only, no assertion changed).
- `tools/dry-run.mjs <backup.json> [--no-amounts]`: in-memory chain run, writes nothing; amount-free tables then an amounts section
  (hidden by `--no-amounts`); exits 1 on any failed check, 2 on a read error.
- Shapes: legacy balances and goal `saved` stay dollars; `mojo.starter`, step `amount`, `goalCents`, `savedCents`, `bankSetTo` are cents.
- Tests: 7 cases (T20, T21 edges, placeholder/empty, no id or role, abort, new setups, afterImport), 59 assertions.
  `run-cases` 681 to 740; `tests.html` 877 to 936.
