# F4 Goal planner (pure)

- New `src/calc/goal-planner.js`. Pure core `planGoals({goals, fundsCountedCents, payDates, today, smilePerPayCents, splurgePerPayCents, unallocatedPerPayCents})` and `planGoalsFromData(data, {today, date, changes, resolve, payDates, unallocatedPerPayCents})`, which resolves scenario changes through `resolve` (default M0's `resolveAt` from `scenario.js`, injectable for tests), reads leaf `bucketGoals` under Smile, `getBuckets` (E5) and `payDatesBetween` (B1).
- `requiredPerPay`: highest over deadlines of (cumulative cost less funds counted) / pays on or before it, rounded up to the cent. Undated goals are listed in `undated`; a goal with money needed and no pay left is `impossible`.
- Gap = required less the Smile per pay. Closed in order: unallocated, then Splurge, then the earnings gap. Options `cut` (capped at Splurge and the gap), `mix` (35% of Splurge to the nearest A$10, `MIX_SHARE_PCT`, U-F11), `earn`. Each option lists `adjustments`, kinds only `unallocated`, `splurge`, `earnings`: Daily and Fire extinguisher are not inputs and never targeted (FT-R-35, `LOCKED_ROLES`). `bigCut` is true strictly above 50% of Splurge. `tickSplurgeItems` (biggest first) and `splurgePlanItems(data)`.
- `grossForExtraNet(data, extraNetPerPayCents)`: bisection on the primary source's annual gross in cents through `netPerPayCents` (C2/B1 engine, tax, Medicare, HELP). Gives the smallest extra gross whose take-home rise reaches the figure. Null when the figure is not positive or a payslip override pins the net. `earningsThreeWays` returns take-home, before tax per pay and per year, hours (1 dp, rate from `hourlyRate`, else gross over hours). On the sample, A$100 more take-home a fortnight needs A$188.67 before tax (about 0.53, the HELP effect NOTES-C predicted; the mock's flat 0.68 is not used).
- Counting (FT-R-93): every Smile bucket balance always; any other bucket or an asset only through an active `goalFund` change (`target:{collection:'allocation'|'assets', id}`; `value:false` or `op:'remove'` switches it off). `fundsCounted`, `goalFundTicks`. UNVERIFIED against F1: the `goalFund` change shape is read from plan section 2.1; if F1 stores it differently, only `goalFundTicks` changes.
- `explainRequired(plan)`: lines "A$9,456.16 still needed", "15 pays", "A$630.42 a pay", from the same cents as the result. Wording is in `EXPLAIN_TEXT` in this file (not the catalogue, to avoid a clash on `en-f.js` in wave 13); F8a may move it.
- Plan figure that was wrong (U-F): F4.1 says Cut Splurge is an **83.4%** cut. 286.58 / 343.84 = 0.83347, which is **83.3%**. Test asserts 83.3. Every other dagger figure (630.42, 244.52, 0, gap 286.58, 57.26, 120.00, 166.58; pays 3, 8, 15) reproduces. Fixture worked back from the mock: funds 1,543.84; concert 1,000 (13 Dec), bike 2,500 (1 Mar), Japan 7,500 (29 May); 9,456.16 / 15 = 630.4107 up to 630.42; 1,956.16 / 8 = 244.52 exactly.
- Not handled: month-mode pay (no anchor) has no pay dates, so dated goals come back `impossible` unless `payDates` is passed. "Date or funding-order moves" are F8a's one-tap options, which re-run `planGoals` with changed goals; this module only absorbs unallocated money.
- Tests: group `goal-planner`, 7 cases (F4.1 to F4.6, F4.7 injected resolver), 76 assertions, including U38 (net per pay monotone with no jump across a A$30k to A$220k sweep through the HELP range) and the Daily/Fire lock across all three options on 48 inputs. `run-cases` 2007 to 2083; `tests.html` 2447 all passed.
