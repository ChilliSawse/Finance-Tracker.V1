# B7 cycle aggregates and the Today / Activity selectors

- `spend-cache.js`: now holds the current pay cycle (`cycle`, `cycleRows`, `cycleByCategory`, `cycleByBucket`, `toReview`)
  and the loaded `rows` (from Jan 1 or 62 days back, whichever is earlier). New `loadSpendCache(rows, {data, today})`
  (sync, used by tests) and `refreshSpendCache({data, today})`. Year and month aggregates unchanged. Cycle and bucket
  figures come from `leftThisPay`; `cycle.end` is exclusive, so a row on it is the next cycle's.
- New `src/state/today-model.js`: pure `buildTodayModel` / `buildActivityFeed`, and the readers `todayModel()` and
  `activityFeed(range)` (`{from,to}`, `{days}`, or the current cycle). Both return `{ready:false}` until the cache loads.
  A matched bill shows once, as its covered transaction; unpaid occurrences show as Upcoming, Due or Missed.
- `main.js`: refresh passes `store.financeData` (comment only otherwise).
- To review (FT-R-55): rows with `status:'review'` in the cycle; confirmed rows and categorised `ok` rows do not count.
- Tests B7.1 to B7.3 (32 assertions). `run-cases` 887 to 919.
